<h1>FOSS4G:UK 2026 Location</h1>

<img src="../assets/images/logo.svg" alt="FOSS4G:UK 2026 Leeds logo" style="max-height:200px; margin: auto; display: block;">

Welcome back to [Horizon Leeds](https://horizonleeds.co.uk/), an award winning, leading-edge conference learning and meeting space which champions new technology, superb locally sourced catering and vibrant, creative spaces full of natural daylight.
After a successful conference last year, we are back with this venue to hold the 2026 year conference.

We have chosen this modern and fully-accessible venue in Leeds City Centre as venue for FOSS4G:UK 2026.

![Image](https://horizonleeds.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/HORIZON_LOGOTYPE_FULLCOLOUR-394px.jpg)

We have booked the entire venue for the conference days and looking forward to welcome up to 220 delegates. The [Create@](https://horizonleeds.co.uk/conference-rooms-leeds/create/) space allows everyone to see our keynote presentations. In addition to the [Inspire@](https://horizonleeds.co.uk/meeting-rooms-leeds/inspire/) and [Think@](https://horizonleeds.co.uk/meeting-rooms-leeds/think/) spaces we have the option to divide the main forum and provide as many of you as possible the opportunity to present engaging talks and workshops.

![Image](https://horizonleeds.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Create@1-carousel-2-800x337.jpg)

Horizon being part of the NHS Confederation means that any profit generated by the venue will support vital health services.

The [Hilton Leeds City](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/leehnhn-hilton-leeds-city/) is located next to Leeds Station.
